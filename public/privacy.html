1. Overview

StockAI ("we," "our," or "us") is a stock analysis application that provides AI-powered market insights, stock data, and investment research tools. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely).

Usage data: We collect information about how you interact with the app, including features used, stocks searched, and session duration, to improve the app experience.

Device information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and app version for diagnostic and support purposes.

Payment information: If you subscribe to a paid tier, payment is processed by Google Play. We do not store your full payment card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the StockAI service
  • To authenticate your account and manage subscriptions
  • To deliver AI-generated stock analysis and market data
  • To send important service updates and notifications
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: Third-party services that help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics), bound by confidentiality agreements

5. Financial Data

StockAI displays financial market data for informational purposes only. We do not provide investment advice. Stock data, AI analysis, and market insights are for research purposes and should not be considered financial advice.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS) and secure password hashing. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

StockAI is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Request deletion of your account and data

The app may display content from third-party financial data providers. These providers have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.
